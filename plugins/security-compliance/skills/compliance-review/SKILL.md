# Compliance Review

Use this skill to guide security and compliance review work from scoping through control assessment, evidence analysis, and remediation planning.

## Load References

- Read `references/control-review-checklist.md` when reviewing current controls, missing safeguards, and likely framework gaps.
- Read `references/audit-evidence-guide.md` when the work depends on audit readiness, evidence collection, ownership, or recurring reviews.

## Core Workflow

1. Define the system boundary, sensitive assets, and relevant framework expectations.
2. Separate implemented controls from intended or undocumented controls.
3. Prioritize issues by risk, exposure, and audit relevance.
4. Distinguish technical gaps, policy gaps, and evidence gaps.
5. Recommend remediation that teams can stage and actually operate.
6. End with ownership, verification, and evidence follow-up.

## What To Prioritize

- Identity and access control
- Secrets handling and key management
- Logging, auditability, and traceability
- Data protection, encryption, and retention
- Secure change management and deployment safeguards
- Incident response readiness and control review cadence

## Review Guidance

- Favor practical risk reduction over generic compliance wording.
- Call out where compensating controls may be acceptable.
- Avoid claiming compliance when evidence is incomplete.
- Note trade-offs when stronger controls affect operability, cost, or delivery speed.

## Output Expectations

When producing recommendations, organize them into:

1. Scope and assumptions
2. Current controls and strengths
3. Key gaps and risks
4. Evidence or documentation weaknesses
5. Prioritized remediation plan
6. Ownership and follow-up actions
